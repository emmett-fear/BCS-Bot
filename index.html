How BCS+ works

BCS+ = 25% AP + 25% Coaches + 25% Computers + 25% Marbles

AP and Coaches are normalized from poll points. The computer component uses the six classic BCS systems: Anderson & Hester, Billingsley, Colley, Massey, Sagarin, and Wolfe. When all six are available, the highest and lowest computer scores are dropped and the other four are averaged.

Marbles uses the official College Football Marbles standings. Raw marble holdings are normalized against the current leader, so the score preserves the gap between teams instead of treating every adjacent rank as equally far apart.

Classic BCS

The original comparison remains unchanged: one-third AP, one-third Coaches, and one-third computer composite. AP stands in for the Harris Poll in this simulation.

Some computer systems do not publish early in the season. BCS+ uses the computer systems available that week. If Marbles data is unavailable for a team, BCS+ is not calculated for that team rather than treating missing data as zero.

Independent simulation. Not affiliated with the BCS, AP, USA TODAY, or College Football Marbles.
