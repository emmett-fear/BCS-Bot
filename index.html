▶ How BCS+ Works

BCS+  =  25% AP  +  25% Coaches  +  25% Computers  +  25% Marbles

AP and Coaches are normalized from poll points. The computer component uses the six classic BCS systems: Anderson & Hester, Billingsley, Colley, Massey, Sagarin, and Wolfe. When all six are available, the highest and lowest computer scores are dropped and the other four are averaged; early in the season, whichever systems have actually published that week are used instead.

Marbles uses the official College Football Marbles standings. Raw marble holdings are normalized against the current leader, so the score preserves the size of the gap between teams instead of treating every adjacent rank as equally far apart.

Classic BCS

The original comparison rides alongside it, unchanged: one-third AP, one-third Coaches, one-third computer composite. AP stands in for the old Harris Poll in this simulation.

If Marbles data isn't available for a team — including every week before Marbles enters the picture — BCS+ is left blank for that team rather than treating the missing data as a zero.

Independent simulation for analysis and entertainment. Not affiliated with the BCS, AP, USA TODAY, any computer ranking provider, or College Football Marbles.
